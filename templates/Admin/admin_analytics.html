Metric Definitions
Credit metrics are per-student averages from catalog credits on scheduled courses, grouped by semester/year/year-of-study and split by General, Male, and Female.
GPA metrics use weighted GPA: sum(grade points × credits) / sum(credits), where A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, F=0.
Course/Faculty/Period-Day grade metrics are average grade points by dimension.
Insights include major/minor popularity, electives, retakes, faculty preference, demand trends, and AI-informed guidance.
Filters apply to Year, Semester, Major, Minor, Gender, and Year of Study; charts auto-refresh as data changes.
